About Me

I am a sophomore at the Colorado School of Mines, majoring in Geophysical Engineering with a focus on climate geophysics and a minor in Applied Mathematics and Statistics. My work centers on using mathematical modeling, numerical methods, and data analysis to study how physical Earth systems respond to forcing over time.
